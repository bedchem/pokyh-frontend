## What the owner asked for

A Spotify-Wrapped-like recap: made in the admin panel for everyone, chosen
schools, classes or single students and a period; showing hours in school,
absences and other WebUntis figures plus what POKYH keeps; a different
mascot reaction per figure (e.g. 0 % vs. over 25 % absences); smooth
"Weiter". During the work: opens by itself at sign-in, closed it goes to a
banner beside the search; "seen" is kept on the POKYH server, not the device.

## Backend (`pokyh-backend`)

- Schema (additive, applied by the usual schema sync): `recaps`,
  `recap_target_schools`, `recap_target_classes` (school + WebUntis class id),
  `recap_target_users`, `recap_views` (account opened / watched to the end;
  nothing the story showed).
- `src/services/recapRules.ts` (pure): who a recap reaches (its school; no
  recipients = every student; otherwise chosen schools, classes, students in
  any mix; parents only when chosen by name), sections, status, covered period,
  "top n %" (only among ≥ 10 people). Tests: `tests/recaps.test.mjs`.
- `src/services/recaps.ts`: the POKYH figures of a period from data already
  kept (request_account_days, usage_sessions with consent, page views with
  consent, archived_activity for archived years, todos/reminders/comments/
  ratings created then, favourite dish), and the place in the school by active
  days (cached 15 min). `summarizeActivity` takes archived days (optional input);
  `mergeSignIns` exported.
- `src/routes/recaps.ts`: `/api/admin/recaps` (list with reach/opened/finished,
  targets, create, update with "show as new again", enable, delete; audited) and
  `/recaps` (list, one recap with POKYH figures, `POST /:id/view`). A recap meant
  for others is never handed out.
- Permissions `recaps.view` / `recaps.manage` (school-scoped, group "Popups &
  Recaps"); `recaps.view` added to the Viewer preset. Admin live area `recaps`.
  Data export lists the account's recap views. `knownClasses` exported from
  `routes/popups.ts`.
- Admin panel: "Recaps" under Inhalte (`pages/RecapsPage.tsx`): title, period
  with quick picks, visible from/until, parts of the story, recipients
  (`components/recaps/RecapRecipientPicker.tsx`: schools, classes per school,
  students), outline of the story's pages, list with status and counts.

## Frontend (`pokyh-frontend`)

- `lib/recap.ts` (API, store, auto-open rule: only while no device was shown
  it — from the server), `lib/recap-data.ts` (loads the period's timetable
  weeks, absences, Fehlzeiten, grades, exams, homework, class register with the
  student's session; weeks kept in `pokyh_recapweeks_<student>`),
  `lib/recap-figures.ts` (pure figures), `lib/recap-story.ts` (pure pages,
  tiers and mascot moods), `lib/i18n/dictionaries/recap.ts` (de, en, it).
  `lib/api.ts`: range fetches for Fehlzeiten and absences.
- `components/recap/`: `RecapStory.tsx` (full-screen story: text rises with
  blur-to-sharp, numbers count up, background colours cross over, one mascot
  walks between places, speech bubble, particles, absence meter with the 25 %
  line, summary with a 1080×1920 share picture), `reactions.ts` (22 moods: e.g.
  crown + star eyes + somersault + confetti at 0 %, hands over eyes + "!" +
  alarm light at ≥ 25 %), `themes.ts`, `share.ts`, `RecapHost.tsx` (loads,
  preloads the figures, opens by itself once nothing else is on screen;
  banner/ring in the top bar; closing flies the card into it).
- `components/app/AppShell.tsx`: host and banner. `next.config.ts`: `recaps`
  in the development proxy.
- Development only: `/de/dev-recap/` (`app/[lang]/dev-recap/page.dev.ts`,
  `components/recap/RecapPreview.tsx`) plays the story with made-up figures for
  every tier; not part of the static build.

## Second round (owner: global admin can't see Recaps; flat pixel design; phone; end card; sharing)

- Rights: a grant stores its permission list, so the new `recaps.*` reached
  only owners. `src/security/permissionUpgrades.ts` (pure, tested in
  `tests/permissions.test.mjs`) and `migrateNewPermissions` (services/
  multiSchool.ts, run once at start after the legacy admin migration, marked
  `admin-grants-recaps-v1`): Global-/School-Admin grants get the new rights of
  their scope; grants with `popups.view` get `recaps.view`, with `popups.manage`
  both. A right taken away later stays away.
- Design, app and admin: no gradients, glow or glass. Flat pixel scenes
  (`components/recap/themes.ts`: sky with a dot pattern, a tiled ground the
  mascot stands on), corners cut in steps, motion in steps. Big figures and
  names in a 5 × 7 pixel font drawn as SVG paths (`pixelFont.ts`,
  `PixelText.tsx`; tests in `tests/recap.test.mjs`). Admin's story outline flat.
- Page turns: block wipes on a canvas (`pixelWipe.ts`, nine patterns:
  sweep, dissolve, spiral, iris, blinds, rain, snake, checker, diamond); the
  story opens out of blocks. Absence meter in 20 coloured blocks; over 25 %
  blinking hazard stripes instead of a red glow.
- Top bar: the banner only from 768 px; on phones only the circle (the module's
  `display` had overridden the `hidden md:flex` utilities). Flat banner, dashed
  ring turning while new.
- End card (`ShareCard.tsx`, `RecapSummary.tsx`): five pixel designs (Zeugnis,
  Arcade, Sammelkarte, Handheld, Poster), ten mascot poses, poster colours,
  which figures and whether the name shows; "PNG" saves 1080 × 1920
  (`share.ts`, the card's SVG at six times), "Teilen" passes the picture with a
  line of text and the site's address to the share sheet; without one,
  WhatsApp's web share, copying, saving.
- Verified in Chrome via the preview page (desktop and a 390 × 844 frame):
  wipes, all pages, all designs, the sheet, the PNG and the share payload
  (intercepted, nothing downloaded or sent). The development server had
  crashed (worker exceptions on every page); it was started again with
